Caz Morning Brief

Thursday, October 29, 2026

Good morning, it's Thursday. Two things for this Saturday, Halloween. Lincklaen Street will be closed to traffic between William and Corwin Streets from 6 to 8 p.m. as a trial for trick-or-treating, and police will start turning cars away at 5:30. Also, if you have a kayak on the racks at Lakeside or Lakeland Park, it needs to come off by Saturday. Looking ahead, next Monday the 2nd is a busy night for local government. The village board holds a public hearing at 7 at 90 Albany Street on banning truck Jake brakes, after noise complaints near Routes 13 and 20. At 7:30, the town board meets at the Gothic Cottage, 7 Albany Street, to adopt the 2027 budget, an increase of about two and a half percent. Tuesday the 3rd is Election Day. Thursday the 5th is the last day of village leaf and brush pickup. Bagged leaves aren't accepted, keep brush separate, and pile limbs at the curb, no longer than 10 feet or thicker than 6 inches. That same evening at 5, ten Madison County businesses compete for 5,000 dollars at a free pitch night in Coleman Lecture Hall on the former college campus. On Tuesday the 10th, from 5:30 to 7 at the American Legion Post on Chenango Street, about 25 applicants present ideas for the village's 10 million dollar downtown revitalization grant, and a survey on the proposals opens that day. On Veterans Day, Wednesday the 11th, Legion Post 88 rededicates the Memorial Cannon at Memorial Park at 11, with a luncheon after for all veterans and families. And on Thanksgiving morning, the Preservation Foundation's Turkey Trot starts at 9:30 on the Gorge Trail. In news, the winter overnight parking ban is in effect on village streets, and cars can be towed during snow events. And congratulations to Cazenovia girls tennis, regional champions headed to the state final four.
